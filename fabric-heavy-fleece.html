Heavy Fleece

A thick, warm sweatshirt fabric designed to provide extra insulation, structure and a substantial premium feel.

Heavy fleece features a knitted construction with a brushed interior that creates a soft, insulating layer. Its higher fabric weight gives garments more body and helps retain warmth, making it especially suitable for cold-weather collections and heavyweight streetwear.

Structure
Heavyweight knitted fleece with a brushed interior and smooth outer face
Composition
Usually 100% cotton, cotton-polyester blends or recycled polyester blends
Weight
320–450 gsm typical
Width
160–190 cm
Handfeel
Thick, soft, warm and substantial
Best for
Heavyweight hoodies, sweatshirts, joggers, winter sets and premium streetwear

Fabric specifications may vary depending on material composition, construction and finishing requirements. Custom weights, widths and fabric blends are available upon request.
