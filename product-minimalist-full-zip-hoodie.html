Minimalist Full-Zip Hoodie

A stripped-back zip hoodie on a 440 gsm cotton-poly, with nothing on the body but the zip and a contrast lining inside the hood.

Wholesale US$11.10 / pc · MOQ 100 pcs

Everything that usually sits on a hoodie has been taken off: no kangaroo pocket, no drawcord, no chest seam. What is left is a clean two-panel hood, a full-length zip on a self-fabric tape, rib cuffs and a rib hem band. The one accent is inside — the hood is lined in a contrasting colour that shows when the hood is down.

The shell is a 440 gsm 65% cotton / 35% polyester with a bonded 100% polyester lining, which is a heavy, dense cloth; it hangs straight rather than clinging. Because the body is unbroken, this is the easiest of our hoodies to put a large back print or embroidery on. Three shell-and-lining combinations are stocked.
