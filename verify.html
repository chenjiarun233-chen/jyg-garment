Why the registered district and the factory district are different. Our business licence is registered in Yuexiu District (administrative code 440104, visible in the credit code itself), while the workshop is in Baiyun District. This is normal for Chinese manufacturers: the registration authority is where the company was incorporated, not where the plant is. If you see two Guangzhou addresses on two different platforms, that is the reason — the Baiyun address is where goods are actually cut, sewn and packed.
